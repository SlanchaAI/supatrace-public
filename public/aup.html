Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026.

SupaTrace is provided by Slancha, Inc., 2930 Domingo Ave, Berkeley, CA 94705, USA. Contact for legal, privacy, sales and support: support@supatrace.dev. Security reports: security@supatrace.dev.

Authorized traces only

Use SupaTrace only for lawful purposes and data you are authorized to process. Obtain required permissions for prompts, outputs, tool results and personal information in traces. Review uploads and imports, minimize personal data and remove secrets. Do not upload stolen credentials, protected health information, unlawful content or data whose processing violates another person's rights.

Free organizations

Do not submit sensitive or regulated data (including protected health information, payment card data, government IDs, credentials or secrets) to a Free organization. Upgrade to a paid plan for the no-training commitment.

Protect the service and other users

Do not gain unauthorized access, probe other customers' data, distribute malware, send spam, facilitate fraud or harassment, bypass access controls or quotas, or disrupt the service through abusive traffic. Do not evade plan limits through misleading identities or unauthorized keys. Keep ingest, source and management credentials private, scoped to their intended project and revoked when no longer needed. Security research must avoid accessing others' data or disrupting service; report concerns to security@supatrace.dev.

Responsibility and enforcement

You are responsible for authorized users, selected import scopes and export destinations. Slancha may restrict, suspend or terminate use that violates this policy under the Terms of Service. We will provide notice where practicable; urgent security or legal concerns may require immediate action. Report abuse or request review of a restriction at support@supatrace.dev. We will announce material policy changes by email or on the site.
