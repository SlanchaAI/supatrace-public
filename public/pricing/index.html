Pricing

Free to start.
Priced by spans.

A free plan with no card, then two flat monthly plans that raise your spans, retention and seats. Base and Pro are monthly subscriptions per organization, charged by Stripe.

Past the allowance

Free and Base stop at the monthly allowance until the next month. Pro has a spend cap that is on by default and does the same. A Pro owner can turn the cap off, and uploads then continue past the allowance at the rate below.

PlanIncluded each monthPast the allowance, cap off
Pro5M spans$5 per 1M spans

Overage is counted and shown in Usage. With the Pro spend cap off, accepted spans above the allowance are billed at $5 per million additional spans.

What applies today

  • Plans: Free plan with no card. Base is $9.99/month. Pro is $25.99/month.
  • Free allowance, enforced: 50k spans per UTC calendar month and 1 GB stored, per organization. A span is one accepted trace event. The span count resets on the first of the month; stored bytes do not reset. At either limit uploads are rejected with an error that names the limit and links to Billing. Nothing already stored is dropped.
  • Count limits, enforced: members, environments and API keys per organization. A create request over the limit is refused; existing items stay.
  • Payments: Stripe processes Base and Pro subscriptions. The paid plan unlocks its limits and features after Stripe confirms payment.
  • Service-wide cap: Shared service caps can stop an upload before an organization reaches its own allowance. Contact support if a service cap blocks your paid plan.
  • Retention, enforced: 7 days on Free, counted from when a batch is accepted. After that it is no longer listed, opened or exported. Expired data is hidden immediately; the enabled scheduled worker erases payload versions and releases stored-byte capacity. Data stored before retention existed does not expire.
  • Projects: projects are isolated within each organization: Free 2, Base 3, Pro 10, Enterprise 20. Billing and usage are shared within the organization.
  • Batches: 100 events / 512 KiB per batch.
  • Keys: write-only ingest keys, revocable, with no expiry unless you set one. Keys made with a setup code expire after 30 days.
  • Export: you can export your original JSON from the workspace on every plan.

Questions

Where do I choose a plan?

Choose Base or Pro in Billing. Stripe collects payment and the plan applies after payment is confirmed.

What happens at the allowance?

New uploads are rejected with an error that names the limit and links to Billing, until the first of the next month (UTC) or until you upgrade. Nothing already stored is dropped.

What happens if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Members, environments and keys over the new limit stay, and you cannot add more until you are under it. Features of the higher plan are locked again.

What is a span?

One recorded step in a trace, such as a model call or a tool call. The service counts one accepted trace event as one span.

Do you charge per member?

No. Each plan includes a number of members per organization.

How is Enterprise priced?

By contract. There is no self-serve checkout: contact sales and an operator assigns the plan to your organization.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes, on every plan. You can export your original JSON from the workspace. Review exports before sharing: traces can contain prompts, tool output and secrets.
