Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026.

SupaTrace is provided by Slancha, Inc., 2930 Domingo Ave, Berkeley, CA 94705, USA. Contact for legal, privacy, sales and support: support@supatrace.dev. Security reports: security@supatrace.dev.

Information we collect

We collect account identifiers, email addresses, authentication and session records, organization membership and settings, keys as digests, trace indexes and trace content you submit, usage and billing records, support messages, and operational/security diagnostics such as request metadata and IP addresses. Traces may contain prompts, model outputs, tool results, personal information and secrets. You choose what to send or authorize for import; review and minimize content before submission.

GitHub sign-in requests user:email; Google sign-in requests openid email. We use provider identity and verified email information for authentication and supported account linking. Email/password and organization single sign-on are also supported where enabled. Session cookies and browser storage support authentication, security and workspace state. We do not use customer traces for advertising.

Purposes and disclosures

Slancha processes customer trace data to provide SupaTrace: storage, indexing, display, authorized imports, export, retention and deletion. For Free organizations only, we also use traces and usage data for the purposes described below. We use account, usage and operational information to authenticate users, enforce access and quotas, prevent abuse, operate billing, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations. Organization owners and members may access content according to their roles and scopes. You control membership and any destinations you configure for exports or drains.

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The provider list explains hosting on AWS in the United States, email through AWS SES, Stripe billing, GitHub and Google authentication, and Cloudflare DNS. Authentication and payment providers receive the information needed for those functions, not trace content from us. A provider you choose to import from or export to receives the scope you authorize; it is not automatically a SupaTrace subprocessor. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or security, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice obligations.

Free plan: use of traces for model training, product improvement and analytics

For Free organizations only, Slancha may use the following categories of information submitted to or generated by those organizations:

  • Trace content, including prompts, model outputs and tool results.
  • Usage data.
  • Account identifiers as needed for the business purposes below.

The business purposes are (a) training and improving machine-learning models, (b) operating, developing and improving SupaTrace and Slancha products, and (c) producing aggregate or de-identified analytics and benchmarks.

On paid plans (Base, Pro and Enterprise), we do not use traces from paid organizations to train or improve models. Upgrading to a paid plan applies the paid-plan commitment to traces accepted after the upgrade. The Terms of Service grant the Free-plan license. The Free-plan license survives an upgrade or closure for data already used before the upgrade or closure. Upgrading or closing an organization does not undo prior uses, including model training.

Do not submit sensitive or regulated data (including protected health information, payment card data, government IDs, credentials or secrets) to a Free organization. Upgrade to a paid plan for the no-training commitment.

Retention, control and deletion

Customers control uploaded content and retention through their plan: 7 days on Free, 30 on Base, 90 on Pro and 365 on Enterprise, with configured custom Enterprise retention. Retention is stamped on acceptance; plan changes do not rewrite existing timestamps. Data stored before retention existed does not expire automatically. Expired data is hidden immediately, then physically erased by the scheduled worker, including payload versions.

Every plan includes workspace JSON export, subject to documented export bounds. Account, organization and project deletion end access immediately and queue physical erasure by the scheduled worker after a ten-minute in-flight grace period. Work is bounded and may retry before completion. Owned organizations must be transferred or deleted before account closure; support can assist with billing-bound organization closure. Individual-trace deletion is unavailable. A minimal non-login identity tombstone remains to prevent reopening closed identities. Recovery backups expire within 35 days. Application logs are kept for 7 days; account, security and billing records may be kept as needed to provide the service, prevent abuse or satisfy legal obligations.

Requests and security

If your data belongs to an organization's traces, contact that organization first; we assist it in handling requests. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to know, correct or delete information, restrict processing, or complain to a privacy regulator. We do not discriminate for exercising applicable rights. No provision here waives rights the law gives you.

We use access controls and encrypted transport and storage as described on the security page. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit protected health information or content you are not authorized to process. Report security concerns to security@supatrace.dev. SupaTrace is intended for adults and not directed to children.
