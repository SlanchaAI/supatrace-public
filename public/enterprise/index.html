Enterprise

Enterprise,
stated plainly.

Enterprise is priced by contract, like the plan it is modelled on. There is no self-serve checkout: you talk to sales and an operator assigns the plan to your organization. This page separates what the plan includes today from what is still by contract or not built.

No SLA document or support commitment is published yet. The features marked available below are built and are switched on for an organization on the Enterprise plan. Compare all plans.

What Enterprise adds

  • SAML single sign-on for your organization, with verified email domains and an option to require it.
  • SCIM user provisioning.
  • Audit log export as JSON or CSV.
  • Custom roles and a rule that requires two-factor authentication.
  • Custom retention and volume set per contract.
  • An uptime SLA and priority support. Terms are pending: no uptime percentage, service credit or response time has been decided. Until terms are published, SupaTrace makes no availability or support commitment.
